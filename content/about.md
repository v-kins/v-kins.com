These are field notes by Timothy Watkins, published under V-Kins. They are observations gathered while building products, leading teams, and trying to make sense of technology. The goal is not to teach. The goal is to share what I have noticed.

Each note is one thing noticed, the context it was noticed in, and what I would do differently. They come from ERP implementations, leading product and engineering teams, listening to customers, reading telemetry, and lately working alongside AI.

It is a personal research database that happens to be published. Notes are captured privately first, and only the ones that pass one rule ever appear here: **patterns yes; people, customers, deals and confidential conversations never.** No identifiable colleagues or company internals. If a note names a vendor's product, it is because the pattern needs it, not to praise or complain. The [Now page](/now/) says what is currently capturing attention.

## The name

V-Kins is the family, not the field notes. Four flags, five kin, one crew. The mark carries the story; these notes carry the author's observations.

## How to read it

Every note has a type: an **observation** is something noticed, a **lesson** is something learned the hard way, a **surprise** is something that went against expectation, a **changed mind** is a position I no longer hold, and a **field note** is a straight report. Tags group notes by topic. There is an [RSS feed](/index.xml), which is the best way to follow along; there is no newsletter and no comments.
